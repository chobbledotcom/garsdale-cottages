# Arrival Information

# Planning your arrival

# - Access Codes/Instructions

 - A key to the front door is located in a key safe within the entrance porch
   
 - We’ll provide you with an access key code before your arrival.This will allow you to check yourself into the
   property. Please make sure you have received the code before you travel. If you haven’t received this before
   setting off, please contact us. There's nothing worse than arriving somewhere new (potentially at night) and
   not being able to get in.

 - We will however likely be home to meet and greet you on arrival and show you around.Just let us know roughly
   what time you expect to arrive

 - Please lock the door whenever you go out and leave the key within the key safe on your final departure
   
# - Check in time

 - Your check-in time is 16.00
   
 - We can sometimes arrange an early check-in,s o please contact us if you plan to arrive early and we'll do our

 - If you are arriving early, have a look at the “Local Area Guide” section. We've listed lots of things you

# Driving  directions

- The address of the property is Roger Pot, Garsdale, Sedbergh, Cumbria, LA10 5PQ
  
- From Sedbergh follow the A684 in the direction of Hawes. Continue for 6 miles, just before you cross the
bridge heading into Garsdale, turn in left and there is a metal gate, with the sign 'Roger Pot'. Drive up the track and the house is at the end of the track.  Depending upon your direction of travel the post code slightly overshoots or undershoots the roadside gateway. If you use W3W the gateway can be located at ///pounds.homing.interlude

 - One of the easiest way to get directions is by using the map on our TouchStay guide. Once on the map you'll see a blue house icon bouncing into position. This is where you are staying. Simply click it and then click "get directions". It will open Google Maps and the end point (where you're staying!) will be filled in. You simply need to type in your starting point.
