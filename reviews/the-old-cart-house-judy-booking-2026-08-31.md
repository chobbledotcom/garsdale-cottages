A beautifully renovated, well-appointed small cottage in a quiet location, perfect for an overnight stay for two. The bed was very comfortable and we enjoyed the local treats in the welcome basket.

Disliked: There was little opportunity for our dogs to have a sniff around immediately outside the house, but we found excellent dog walking opportunities only a short walk from the house.
