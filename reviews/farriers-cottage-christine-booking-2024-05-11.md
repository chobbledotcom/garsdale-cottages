Charming cottage in the Dales - with antique furniture and comfy beds. Tiny but well equipped kitchen. Wonderful garden to sit outside.

Disliked: There are areas which need more attention. We found half eaten crisps in the drawers of bedside tables and - this being even dangerous to kids- unfinished Packets of medication. We didn't mind a bitod dust here and there, but the dishes kept in open shelves needed to be washed before using and the teapot hadn't been washed in a while.
