Very sweet cottage with everything you need for a short stay. The welcome basket of Ale, Biscuits, Cereals, Mint cake and homemade jam was really appreciated. 
The cottage is well appointed and Lorraine has equipped it with all the essentials you need. Lots of instructions to hand to and interesting history of the Old Cart House to read. 
The bed is very comfortable and the shower has plenty of hot water. 
I sat outside in the sunshine looking at the view in the little garden at the back of the cottage. The view is stunning. I ventured out and went for a scenic walk in the surrounding dales and alongside the river. Parking is directly outside the cottage on the road. The road is busy but it is high season.
