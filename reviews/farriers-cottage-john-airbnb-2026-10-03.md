Ferrier's Cottage is an idyllic country retreat. Excellent in its design and renovated sufficiently to accomodate two couples that lend to privacy for all. It is well-appointed with amenities and conveniences, and charmingly furnished with many family antiques. It has all the desireable aspects of an escape to the country. By the same token, it is relatively remote, requires the use of a car to come and go, and to venture further afield for groceries, pubs, attractions and other needs. We highly recommend this property and look forward to returning in the future.
