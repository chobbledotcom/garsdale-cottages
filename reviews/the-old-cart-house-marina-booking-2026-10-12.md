Location was superb. Peaceful, only sound was the trickling river over the road. Key was easy to retrieve from keysafe box. Hosts there to welcome us. Gorgeous cottage, cosily furnished. Lots of information for area and appliances. Secure for our dogs. It was bags in, kettle on and a cuppa in front of the fire. We were settled within 10 minutes. Bedroom, bed, shower all very good. Lovely little touches throughout the cottage make it homely, there is all you need for a very comfortable self-catering stay.

Disliked: Walking in woodland, we lucked out on seeing a red squirrel. A local gentleman told us where one had been a few minutes earlier. It remained elusive for us.
