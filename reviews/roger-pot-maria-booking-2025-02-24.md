Absolutely stunning location with wonderful views from every part of the property.Good layout with good floor plan, nice and cosy living room with wood burner great for entertaining ,well equipped kitchen leading into the dining room with stunning views.Three good size bedrooms,one with ensuite bathroom ,all bedrooms with super comfortable beds for a great night sleep.Very handy downstairs toilet which is unusual in older properties.Property is pet friends without additional cost which is very rare nowadays to be honest.Nice enclosed garden to the side of the property with stunning views.Great host, very responsive during booking process and very helpful once we were there.Great welcome instructions with all information you would need and recommendations for things to do and visit in the area .Amazing welcome pack left by the owner with amazingly delicious homemade bread and cake and much more.I will definitely visit again if I have a chance to book again as this property is rarely available on booking.com.Price was great for what property and area has to offer.Also property was absolutely spotless clean.

Disliked: Unfortunately there was an issue with underfloor heating on the ground floor when we arrived and back up electric radiators were locked, the owner mentioned to let him know if we need them on when we arrived which we did next day but would be nice to have them unlocked and leave it to guests if they need additional heating or not so they don't have to bother the owner.Living room would benefit from larger TV but I guess in such location TV shouldn't be even on.
