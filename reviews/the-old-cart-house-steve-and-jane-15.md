We loved our stay at The Old Carthouse. It has been carefully and tastefully kitted out with everything you need for a cosy and comfortable stay. We are keen walkers and, although the walking directly from the property is limited, we found it a great place to access excellent routes around dales and towns. We loved the complementary goodies (including local beers), the well-equipped kitchen for cooking, and we had a hearty meal in the Black Bull in Sedbergh. The bed is comfortable and the bathroom / shower is tastefully done. Highly recommended!
