We stayed at Roger Pot for a long weekend and it was not long enough! The home is just beautiful!, it is very well equipped and is cozy.
The Owners are very accommodating and gave us some good ideas on where to take lovely walks. We loved our welcome pack including hike made bread which was such a thoughtful touch. 
The view from Roger Pot is very beautiful, the place is tranquil. We will be visiting again very soon. 

Definitely recommend if you have dogs who enjoy long walks
