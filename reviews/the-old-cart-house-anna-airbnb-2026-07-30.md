We recently stayed at The Old Cart House and had the best few days. <br/>We loved the location and how peaceful and picturesque it was. We found the place extremely relaxing and comfortable. The place looks slightly outdated on the photos but it's nothing like it. The decor is actually brilliant and suits the place perfectly giving it cosy cottage vibes. Loved the wooden staircase and the beams features. Kitchen is rather on a smaller side but has everything you need. Bed is big and comfy. If you're tall you also need to watch your head in few places as you would in most of the old cottages. We both thought Loraine and her husband did a great job renovating this place. <br/>Lorraine herself was a brilliant host. Very friendly, responsive and communication was great.<br/>We would definitely love to stay again 💚
