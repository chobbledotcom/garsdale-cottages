Our stay at The Old Cart House was sadly just a one night stop on a journey between family in the south of England and home in north Scotland but what a perfect stop! A peaceful, quirky and cosy cottage with the lovely sound of a river running just over the road. We had great dog walks from Garsdale Station just after we arrived and from Dandra Garth in the morning. Nearby Sedbergh has a good selection of pubs  for an evening meal. We'll definitely be back on another similar trip but the cottage has persuaded us that a longer stay would be a far better plan!!
