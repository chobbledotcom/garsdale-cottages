          ## Where Garsdale Fits In

          Garsdale is in Cumbria and within the Yorkshire Dales National Park, about six miles east of [Sedbergh](/locations/sedbergh/). The county boundary and the national park boundary are not the same thing. Garsdale is outside the [Lake District](/places/lake-district/), which is a separate national park further west, so a cottage stay here puts you in a farming dale rather than a lakeside town.

          That geography gives you a useful choice of days out. You can stay local for riverside paths, [red squirrels](/red-squirrels-in-garsdale/) and the [Settle-Carlisle railway at Garsdale Station](/places/garsdale-station/), head towards the Howgills, or drive west to Kendal and the southern Lakes. Cumbria covers a large area, and the coast or northern fells should not be treated as equally close to Garsdale.

          ## Sedbergh and the Howgill Fells

          Sedbergh is the nearest town for shops, cafes and bookshops. Allow around fifteen to twenty minutes from the cottages as a rough driving guide, depending on your starting point and road conditions. [Farfield Mill](/places/farfield-mill-sedbergh/) is on the way and has arts, crafts and local textile history, making it a sensible stop when a whole day on the fells would be too much.

          The [Howgill Fells](/places/howgill-fells/) rise above the town, with Winder and The Calf among the familiar walking destinations. The longer routes involve substantial ascent and exposed ground, even though the hills look rounded from below. At the eastern side, [Cautley Spout](/places/cautley-spout/) can be visited on a separate outing, with the [Cross Keys Temperance Inn](/places/cross-keys-temperance-inn-cautley/) nearby. Check opening and meal arrangements before relying on a stop there.

          ## Kendal and the Southern Lakes

          [Kendal](/locations/kendal/) is useful for larger supermarkets as well as a day out. Plan on roughly thirty to forty minutes by car from Garsdale, allowing more for traffic and parking. [Kendal Castle](/places/kendal-castle/) offers a walk above the town, while the [Brewery Arts Centre](/places/brewery-arts-centre-kendal/) has cinema and performances for wetter days. Check its current programme rather than expecting every activity to run daily.

          [Levens Hall](/places/levens-hall/), south of Kendal, is known for its topiary gardens and can be combined with a town visit when opening dates suit. The [Lake District guide](/places/lake-district/) covers Windermere, Ambleside and Grizedale, with journey and parking advice. Allow roughly an hour to the southern Lakes in ordinary conditions, and longer for busy weekends or destinations further into the national park.

          ## Valleys and Railway Journeys

          [Dent village](/places/dent-village/) makes another local outing, with cobbled streets and walks in Dentdale. The scenic roads over the hills are narrow and exposed in places; check the weather and use a route suited to your vehicle. [Dent Station](/places/dent-station/) is several miles from the village, so do not assume a train arrival leaves you in the centre.

          For a different view of the country, look at services from [Garsdale Station](/places/garsdale-station/) towards Kirkby Stephen, Appleby or Carlisle. Check the current timetable and arrange transport to and from the station before choosing a journey. Our [getting here](/getting-here/) page explains the rural transport limitations, and our [walks guide](/walks/) has options that do not require a full day of driving.
