          Jhoots Pharmacy was a pharmacy on Main Street in Sedbergh, at number 48-50. It closed without warning on 21 August 2025, citing a lack of a pharmacist, and the BBC reported that the closure left people in Sedbergh facing a ten-mile journey to the nearest pharmacy. The premises have since reopened as Allied Pharmacy, so Sedbergh has a pharmacy counter again on Main Street - the details in the box alongside are for the Allied Pharmacy now trading there.
          
          If you need a pharmacy while staying in Garsdale, Allied Pharmacy in Sedbergh is the nearest, about fifteen minutes' drive west. [Central Dales Pharmacy in Hawes](/places/central-dales-pharmacy-hawes/) is the alternative about twenty minutes' drive east, and pharmacies in Kendal cover out-of-hours. For minor ailments and over-the-counter medication, [SPAR Sedbergh](/places/spar-sedbergh/) on Station Road sells a basic range. For prescriptions and urgent medicine, [Westmorland General Hospital](/places/westmorland-general-hospital-kendal/) in Kendal has an Urgent Treatment Centre.
