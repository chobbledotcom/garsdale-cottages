          ## Staying Near the Lake District

          Our holiday cottages in Garsdale are in [Cumbria](/places/cumbria/), within the Yorkshire Dales National Park. Garsdale is not inside the Lake District National Park, which lies to the west. A stay here lets you spend most of your time walking in the Dales and the nearby [Howgill Fells](/places/howgill-fells/), with a day in the Lakes when you fancy a change.

          The southern Lake District is the more practical starting point for a day out. Drive west through [Sedbergh](/locations/sedbergh/) towards [Kendal](/locations/kendal/), then follow signs for Windermere and Ambleside. Allow roughly an hour to Windermere or Ambleside as a planning guide, with longer needed in busy periods. The route, your particular cottage, traffic and parking all affect the time, so check current journey information before setting off. Northern and western destinations need a longer day rather than a quick detour.

          ## Windermere, Ambleside and Grizedale

          Windermere town and the lakeside at Bowness are different places, so choose your parking according to what you want to do. A lakeside walk or boat trip can fill a good part of the day; check sailing times, weather and the last return before buying tickets. In summer it is worth arriving early rather than circling for a space at lunchtime.

          Ambleside has outdoor shops, cafes and walks, with the lake shore at Waterhead a little way from the centre. [Zeffirellis](/places/zeffirellis-ambleside/) combines a vegetarian restaurant with a cinema, useful when the weather turns, while [Doi Intanon](/places/doi-intanon-ambleside/) is another option for a meal. Check opening times and book ahead if eating out is part of your plan.

          [Go Ape at Grizedale Forest](/places/go-ape-grizedale-forest/) gives families with older children an alternative to a fell walk. Check age, height and booking requirements for the activity you choose. Grizedale also has forest walking routes, but it is further around the lake than Windermere town and the smaller roads add to the drive. Do not confuse Grizedale Forest in the Lakes with [Grisedale](/grisedale/), the neighbouring valley to Garsdale.

          ## Planning a Hill Day

          Lake District fells need the same care as any mountain day. Choose a route that suits the whole party, carry a map and suitable clothing, and check the [Mountain Weather Information Service Lake District forecast](https://www.mwis.org.uk/forecast/english-and-welsh/lake-district). Poor visibility, wet rock and short winter days can change what is sensible. Our [local walks](/walks/) include nearer options if the drive would leave too little daylight.

          [Honister Slate Mine](/places/honister-slate-mine/) is a longer excursion in the northern Lakes, not something to squeeze in after an afternoon in Ambleside. Check the route, conditions and activity booking arrangements first. For a shorter outing, [Kendal Castle](/places/kendal-castle/) and the [Brewery Arts Centre](/places/brewery-arts-centre-kendal/) are outside the national park and closer to Garsdale.
