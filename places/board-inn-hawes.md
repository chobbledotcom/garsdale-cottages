          ## Eating and Drinking in Hawes with a Dog

          England's highest market town has more pubs, cafes and restaurants than Sedbergh does, and it is worth a day rather than an hour. The Board Inn does both meals and drinks, so it covers an evening out as well as a stop for a pint, and it is a straightforward drive east along the A684 from the dale.

          The other dog friendly options in and around Hawes are the [White Hart](/places/white-hart-inn-hawes/) on the cobbled main street, which keeps one of its three dining areas dog-free and welcomes dogs in the other two, [Caffè Curva](/places/caff-curva/) on Market Place for daytime food, and the [Green Dragon](/places/green-dragon-inn-hardraw/) at Hardraw just outside town, with [Hardraw Force](/places/hardraw-force/) reached through the pub grounds.

          A visit fits well around the Tuesday [market](/places/the-market-house-hawes/) and the craft fairs in the Market House, the [Dales Countryside Museum](/places/dales-countryside-museum-hawes/) in the old railway station, the [Wensleydale Creamery](/places/wensleydale-creamery-hawes/) and its cheese experience, or [W.R. Outhwaite & Son](/places/wr-outhwaite-and-son-hawes/), the rope makers on the high street.

          Food service times move about with the season in Hawes as they do everywhere up here, so ring ahead if you are driving over for a meal.
