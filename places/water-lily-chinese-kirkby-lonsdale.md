          Water Lily is a Chinese restaurant and takeaway on Market Square in Kirkby Lonsdale, serving Cantonese, Szechuan and Hong Kong-style Chinese food, with a menu that covers the usual Chinese restaurant staples alongside fast food options. It does both eat-in dining and takeaway, with the takeaway side being particularly well-reviewed. The restaurant is on the corner of the Market Square, a short walk from [Spice Essence](/places/spice-essence-indian-cuisine/) and [Collinge Opticians](/places/collinge-opticians-hawes/).
          
          Kirkby Lonsdale is about thirty minutes' drive from Garsdale, so it is a fair way for a Chinese, but if you are over that way for the [Thursday Charter Market](/places/market-square-kirkby-lonsdale/) or a walk around the town, it is a handy option.
