          On Time Taxis is a small local taxi company based at Maryfell in Sedbergh, run as a daytime-only operation with one four-seater car - so no multi-seaters or minibuses. It is the kind of outfit that suits a couple or a small family needing a lift from Sedbergh to [Garsdale train station](/places/garsdale-station/), or a rail transfer to Oxenholme, or a pickup from the Dales Way if you are walking and need luggage moved or onward travel arranged.
          
          Because it is a one-car, daytime-only operation, it is worth booking ahead rather than expecting a car at short notice, especially in the walking season. The company also does luggage transfers for walkers along the Dales Way, which is useful if you are doing a multi-day walk and want your bags moved between stops.
