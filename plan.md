## Key New Information from Archives (not yet on site)

### From garsdale-info.md:
1. **Mount Zion Chapel details** - Built by railway contractors Groves & Woodiwiss, painted in Midland Railway colours (maroon/cream), only place of worship built by railway contractors, hireable as walker base (£10/session), views of Dandrymire Viaduct
2. **St John's Church - Wordsworth connection** - In 1799 Wordsworth wrote to Coleridge describing walking through Garsdale with Dorothy, mentioning "a tempting inn, close by a lowly house of prayer"
3. **C.E. Kempe stained glass** at St John's
4. **The Great Flood of 1889** - Baugh Fell burst open, 8 stone bridges and 7 wooden ones destroyed, school flooded to 4ft, Winn family rebuilt bridges then founded building firm in Sedbergh
5. **Literary connections** - "Twixt Moor and Mead" (1887), "The Death of Grass" (1956) by John Christopher, Andrew Lloyd Webber connection via "Aspects of Love"
6. **Garsdale dialect glossary** - addle, bairn, brant, cleg, fettle, ginnel, heeaf, laik, mowdy, neb, smoot, sneck, syke, tewit, thrang
7. **Tom Croft Cave and Dovecote Cave** - local caves to explore
8. **Cotterdale Force** - waterfall sketched by Turner
9. **Dry stone wall styles** - each wall identifies the waller who built it
10. **Garsdale Hall** - formerly the George & Dragon coaching inn (1851 census), Junction Cottage was the Junction Inn
11. **Wensleydale "Garsdale" cheese** - garlic and chives variety
12. **Wild garlic ("Ramps")** grows locally
13. **Viking/Norse heritage** - house names reflect Norse settlement
14. **Annual Grisedale walk** from Mount Zion Chapel (July/Aug) with tea afterwards
15. **Evening walks** from Village Hall in summer (6:15-6:30pm), sometimes with ploughman's supper/barbecue
16. **Specific red squirrel locations** - Reachey, Mudbecks, Railway Cottages, Banks, East Littletown, Paradise, Dandra Garth, Coat Weggs, The Hive, The Firs, Rackenthwaite, Fold Gill, Whitbeck, Nether House
17. **Road crossing warnings** at Banks, The Street, The Firs, Whitbeck

### From garsdale-parish-council.md:
18. **Otters on the River Clough** - confirmed spraints and footprints, EA 2010 survey found positive signs at 70/100 Lune Catchment sites (up from 37 in 2007), naturally re-colonised
19. **Migratory fish and eels** - culvert at Winterscales Farm funded by Lune Rivers Trust
20. **Invasive plants** - Japanese Knotweed and Himalayan Balsam along the Clough
21. **Dark skies / stargazing** - "Stars Above Baugh Fell" photos suggest excellent dark skies
22. **Named landscape features** along A684 - Black Horse Hill, Dandra Garth, Badger Dub, Longstone Fell, Aye Gill, Roger Pot
23. **Dandrymire/Moorcock Viaduct** - notable railway engineering landmark
24. **Moorcock Inn pub quiz** - Tuesday evenings, 8:30pm
25. **Kirk Bridge** - listed bridge near Village Hall
26. **"Bell Pratt of Grisdale"** book - local history about farming life
27. **"Aygill to Foldgill"** - Kevin Lancaster's local history content
28. **Banks Farm** - listed farm building
29. **Garsdale First Responders** - community emergency volunteers

### From garsdale-red-squirrels.md:
30. **Group had 38 members by early 2015**
31. **Threat detail**: habitat loss from forestry clear felling + grey squirrel pox virus
32. **Habitat preference**: reds in coniferous forest, greys in broadleaf/deciduous along river
33. **Snaizeholme Red Squirrel Trail** - dedicated viewing near Hawes
