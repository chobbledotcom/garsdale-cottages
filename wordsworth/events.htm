August Events 2022

August 4th - Lake District Sheep Dog Trials
The Trials start at 10am and will run all day. There are also 3 hound trails at 1pm, 2.15pm and 2.45pm.
With an open Dog Show and pet class at 1pm. There is food, a bar and an ice cream van on site plus a small number of craft stalls and a sheep clipping demonstration.
Event takes place at: Hill Farm, Ings, Nr Kendal, Cumbria, LA8 9QQ
