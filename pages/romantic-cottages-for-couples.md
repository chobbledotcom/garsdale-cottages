          ## A Romantic Cottage for Two in Garsdale

          [The Old Cart House](/properties/the-old-cart-house/) is a detached one-bedroom stone cottage in Garsdale, in the Yorkshire Dales National Park, and it sleeps two, which makes it the holiday cottage in our group that couples book. It was built as a cart house and stable, listed in the 1845 survey as J Davis' stable, and since Jack and Loraine converted it, it has a king-size bed, an en-suite shower room, exposed beams under a vaulted ceiling and a small patio garden at the back with views up the valley. Sedbergh is 6.4 miles down the dale and Hawes is 9 miles the other way.

          The couples who have stayed write about much the same things: how quiet it is at night, the sound of the river over the road, the bed, and the basket of local treats left for them on arrival. Jack and Loraine take one-night stays as well as longer breaks, and you can book either online.

          ## Inside The Old Cart House

          Downstairs is one open-plan room with beams overhead, an electric fire, a dining table for two and a small kitchen fitted in under the staircase, with an oven, hob, microwave and fridge freezer. The timber staircase goes up past a window seat on the half-landing, which looks out over the dale, to the bedroom. The king-size bed has a quilt on it rather than plain hotel white, and a reclaimed stable door opens onto the en-suite, which has a wide walk-in shower.

          A second door from the bedroom opens onto stone steps down to the patio, where there is a table and two chairs among the plant pots. In summer guests eat out there, and on a clear night it is somewhere to sit and look at the stars, since there is no street lighting anywhere in the dale. There is a Smart TV and WiFi, and a shelf of books.

          The welcome basket has had local ale in it, along with biscuits, mint cake and homemade jam, and there is tea and coffee for the morning.

          >
          > *Lyndsey, The Old Cart House*

          ## Evenings In and Evenings Out

          The River Clough runs just across the road and guests have heard it from the cottage, the road itself is quiet at night, and with the electric fire on it is an easy night in. One guest described arriving as bags in, kettle on and a cuppa in front of the fire, and being settled within ten minutes.

          If you do want to eat out, the nearest pubs are a short drive away. The [White Hart](/places/white-hart-inn-hawes/) in Hawes is a 16th-century coaching inn on the cobbled main street, the [Green Dragon](/places/green-dragon-inn-hardraw/) at Hardraw serves food and has the waterfall in its grounds, and the [Black Bull](/places/black-bull-inn-sedbergh/) in Sedbergh does meals and is about fifteen minutes away. The [Sportsman's Inn](/places/sportsmans-inn-cowgill/) at Cowgill is over the hill in Dentdale. There are normally no taxis in Garsdale, and [On Time Taxis](/places/on-time-taxis-sedbergh/) in Sedbergh only runs in the daytime, so for a pub supper in Sedbergh or Hawes plan on one of you driving. Our [eating and drinking](/eating-and-drinking-in-garsdale/) page lists the rest.

          > "We had a lovely meal outside among the flowers and plants."
          >
          > *Christina, The Old Cart House*

          ## Days Out for Couples

          The Buttertubs Pass climbs high and open from Hawes over to Swaledale, and [Muker](/places/muker-village/) and its wildflower meadows are on the far side. [Hardraw Force](/places/hardraw-force/) is England's highest single-drop waterfall and is reached through the Green Dragon. The [Wensleydale Creamery](/places/wensleydale-creamery-hawes/) in Hawes runs cheesemaking demonstrations and tastings, and [Farfield Mill](/places/farfield-mill-sedbergh/), on the road between Garsdale and Sedbergh, has exhibitions, craft work and a cafe. Sedbergh is England's official Book Town, with bookshops and book cafes along Main Street.

          A little further afield, [Kirkby Lonsdale](/locations/kirkby-lonsdale/) has [Devil's Bridge](/places/devils-bridge-kirkby-lonsdale/) and [Ruskin's View](/places/ruskins-view-kirkby-lonsdale/) over the Lune, and [Semerwater](/places/semerwater/) and [Aysgarth Falls](/places/aysgarth-falls/) are both in Wensleydale. You can take the [Settle-Carlisle railway](/places/garsdale-station/) from Garsdale Station over the Ribblehead Viaduct, and the [Lake District](/places/lake-district/) is close enough for a day trip from the Yorkshire Dales, with Ambleside roughly an hour's drive through Sedbergh and Kendal.

          > "It is also a great base to explore the Dales, including completing the drive through the Buttertubs pass."

          ## Anniversaries, Birthdays and Special Occasions

          Guests have come to Garsdale for birthdays and anniversaries, and The Old Cart House takes one-night bookings, so a single night away for a birthday is easy to arrange online without having to book a whole weekend. If the celebration is a family one, [Roger Pot](/properties/roger-pot/) sleeps six, and one family took it to celebrate their parents' anniversary, while another came for a birthday weekend. Paul bakes for every guest at Roger Pot, and guests have found homemade bread and parkin or ginger cake in the welcome hamper, and several have mentioned fresh flowers on the dining table.

          ## Good to Know Before You Book

          The bedroom and the en-suite shower room are both upstairs, and there is no bathroom on the ground floor, so the cottage will not suit anyone who cannot manage stairs. The beams are low in places, and the rear door from the downstairs room is low enough that you will need to mind your head. Parking is at the roadside on the A684 for one car. It is a main road on the map, but most guests have found it quiet during the day and very quiet at night, though one found it busy in high summer.

          One pet is welcome, either a dog or a cat. The garden is not fully enclosed and the cottage is in sheep farming country, so dogs need to stay on a lead when you are out walking and cannot be left in the garden on their own. Our [dog friendly cottages](/dog-friendly-cottages-in-garsdale-or-yorkshire-dales-stays/) page has more. If you have a baby, the cottage takes a travel cot, and our [cottage for a couple with a baby](/cottage-for-a-couple-with-a-baby/) page explains what is provided.
