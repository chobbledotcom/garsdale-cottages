# Dog-Friendly Holiday Cottages in Garsdale & the Yorkshire Dales

Bring your four-legged companion on an unforgettable getaway surrounded by tranquil woodland, riverside trails, and rolling Dales countryside.

## Pawsome Stays in the Heart of the Dales

At Garsdale Cottages, we know that a true family holiday isn't complete without your dog. Nestled in the quiet valley of Garsdale, between Sedbergh and Hawes, our self-catering properties offer the ideal base for walking holidays with your pet.

Enjoy doorstep access to endless trail networks, cozy nights by the fire after a long hike, and peaceful surroundings where your dog is genuinely welcomed, not just tolerated.

## Pet Friendly Etiquette & Safety

To help us keep our cottages welcoming for future pets and protect local livestock, we kindly ask guests to review our house rules before arrival:

> **Countryside Code Reminder:** Garsdale is a working agricultural valley. Please keep dogs on leads near farm animals. You can learn more about our local farming community in our [Farming in Garsdale guide](https://www.garsdalecottages.co.uk/farming-in-garsdale/).

- **Pet Policy:** Please read our full [Pet Rules & Guidelines](https://www.garsdalecottages.co.uk/guide/safety-info-rules/pets/).
- **Packing Checklist:** Remember to bring towels for muddy paws, dog beds, and food bowls. Check our [Things to Bring Guide](https://www.garsdalecottages.co.uk/guide/before-you-leave-home/things-to-bring/) for details.
- **Cleaning Equipment:** Need to wipe down muddy paws after a rainy hike? Find out where hosepipes and towels are kept in our [Cleaning Equipment Guide](https://www.garsdalecottages.co.uk/guide/about-the-accommodation/cleaning-equipment/).
