          ## A Cottage Break off the Motorway

          Garsdale's holiday cottages are around twenty minutes from M6 junction 37 in favourable driving conditions, reached through Sedbergh on the A684. They are in [Cumbria](/places/cumbria/), within the Yorkshire Dales National Park, with walks and countryside around them rather than motorway services outside the door. The time is an estimate from the junction, not a guaranteed arrival time at every cottage: traffic through Sedbergh, weather, farm vehicles and the final access can all add to it.

          If you are travelling north towards Scotland or south towards Lancashire and the Midlands, a stay here can break up the journey and leave time for a walk or a day in the Dales. These are self-catering holiday cottages, not a roadside hotel. Availability, minimum stays, arrival days and check-in times must be checked for your dates before booking. Do not assume a single-night stopover, a same-day booking or a late arrival is available.

          ## From M6 Junction 37 via Sedbergh

          Leave the M6 at junction 37 and take the A684 towards Sedbergh, around five miles away. Continue through Sedbergh following the route towards Kirkby Stephen, then take the A684 right turn signposted Garsdale and Hawes shortly beyond the town. Follow the A684 east up the dale for about another six miles, using the directions supplied by your cottage owner for the final entrance.

          The postcode for [The Old Cart House](/properties/the-old-cart-house/) and [Roger Pot](/properties/roger-pot/) is **LA10 5PQ**, but a shared rural postcode is not a substitute for those final directions. Roger Pot is reached by a gated farm track near the village bridge. The Old Cart House is further east along the road, past the village hall and church. [Farrier's Cottage](/properties/farriers-cottage/) has its own arrival instructions; use the address and access details confirmed for that property.

          Roger Pot's track is about 400 metres long, single width, steep and uneven in places, with gates to open. The owner uses a texting system for movements up and down it. Very low-profile cars may need to remain by the road, so discuss access before choosing the cottage. Our [getting here](/getting-here/) page has fuller directions from both Sedbergh and Hawes.

          ## Before a Northbound or Southbound Stopover

          Confirm your booking and arrival arrangements before leaving the motorway. Bring groceries for your first evening, especially if reaching Garsdale after the shops in Sedbergh have closed, and download the directions because mobile coverage in the dale can be patchy. Check current traffic and weather, and allow enough time for an unhurried final approach after dark.

          If you have time before continuing north or south, [Sedbergh](/locations/sedbergh/) has bookshops and cafes, [Farfield Mill](/places/farfield-mill-sedbergh/) is on the approach to the dale, and our [walks page](/walks/) covers local outings. The [Howgill Fells](/places/howgill-fells/) need more time and preparation than a short roadside stroll. For a longer break, the [Lake District](/places/lake-district/) is a day-trip option to the west; Garsdale itself is outside the Lakes. Our [last minute and one night stays](/last-minute-and-one-night-stays/) page explains how one-night and short-notice bookings work at The Old Cart House.
