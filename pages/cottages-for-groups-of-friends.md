          ## Holiday Cottages for Friends and Couples in Garsdale

          Two of our holiday cottages in Garsdale, in the Yorkshire Dales National Park near Sedbergh, sleep six, and both suit a group of friends or two or three couples going away together. [Roger Pot](/properties/roger-pot/) can be set up with three double bedrooms, so three couples can each have a double bed at this Yorkshire Dales holiday cottage. [Farrier's Cottage](/properties/farriers-cottage/) has a king-size double, a second double and a twin, with a bathroom on each floor. Both are self-catering stone cottages with a woodburning stove, a separate dining room and walks from the door.

          Our [family friendly holidays](/family-friendly-holidays-in-garsdale/) page covers these cottages for families with children. This page is for groups of adults, whether that is old friends, a walking group, couples who holiday together every year, or grown-up children taking their parents away.

          ## Three Couples at Roger Pot

          [Roger Pot](/properties/roger-pot/) is a detached stone cottage a quarter of a mile up a farm track, with three bedrooms and two bathrooms. The master bedroom has a super-king bed, wooden roof beams, low windows with the best of the view and an en-suite walk-in shower. The second bedroom is a double, and the third is a twin zip and link, which Paul will make up as a super-king if you let him know before you arrive, giving three couples a double bed each. The family bathroom has a bath with a shower over it, and the utility room has its own toilet, so there is a downstairs loo as well.

          Downstairs there is a sitting room with a woodburning stove and leather sofas, a kitchen with a dishwasher, induction hob and air fryer, and a separate dining room. The dining table extends, and one New Year group had family staying nearby round for meals, though the visitors had to bring their own chairs. Outside there is an enclosed side garden, and a table with six chairs out on the grass looking over the valley. There are board games and guide books in the house, and two TVs, though the one in the sitting room is on the small side.

          Paul and Karen live in the barn next door, and Paul bakes for every guest, so there is homemade bread and cake waiting when you arrive. Up to two dogs are welcome and there is no charge for them. Because the owners live on site, Roger Pot suits a group who want a quiet few days in the hills rather than a party house.

          ## Two Couples at Farrier's Cottage

          [Farrier's Cottage](/properties/farriers-cottage/) is a semi-detached stone cottage that began life in 1675 as the blacksmith's cottage and workshop. Upstairs there is a king-size double with an iron and brass bedstead, a second double, and a twin up under the old roof beams, with the family bathroom on the same floor. There is also a shower room on the ground floor, so two couples are not queueing for the same bathroom in the morning, and one guest whose party was two couples wrote that the design gave privacy for all.

          The sitting room has a woodburning stove in a big exposed-stone fireplace, with leather sofas and shelves of books and DVDs, and the dining room has a table that seats six, a grandfather clock and a writing bureau. The kitchen has a dishwasher, the utility room has a washing machine and tumble dryer, there is off-road parking for two cars and somewhere dry to put the bikes. Fuel for the stove is included. Phil, who owns it, takes enquiries through the form on the cottage page.

          ## Bigger Groups and All Three Cottages

          If there are more than six of you, the three cottages can be booked together for up to fourteen guests. They are owned separately and are spread along the dale rather than under one roof, so availability has to be coordinated between the owners, who are neighbours. [The Old Cart House](/properties/the-old-cart-house/) sleeps two and works for one couple alongside a larger party in one of the six-berth cottages.           ## What Groups Do Round Here

          For a walking group, the [Howgill Fells](/places/howgill-fells/) above Sedbergh give a good hill day over Winder, Arant Haw and The Calf, [Ingleborough](/places/ingleborough/) and Whernside are within easy reach for anyone doing the Three Peaks a hill at a time, and the Pennine Bridleway runs above Garsdale Head. There are [walks from the door](/walks/) as well, up through the woods at Dandra Garth or over Rise Hill towards Dent.

          For a day off the hills, the [Settle-Carlisle railway](/places/garsdale-station/) from Garsdale Station runs south over the [Ribblehead Viaduct](/places/ribblehead-viaduct/) towards Settle, or north to Carlisle, and the [Wensleydale Creamery](/places/wensleydale-creamery-hawes/) in Hawes does tastings. [Sedbergh Golf Club](/places/sedbergh-golf-club/), a mile outside town, plays as an 18-hole round over nine greens, the [Dales Bike Centre](/places/dales-bike-centre/) in Swaledale hires out mountain bikes, and [Hardraw Force](/places/hardraw-force/) is reached from behind the Green Dragon at Hardraw. The [Brewery Arts Centre](/places/brewery-arts-centre-kendal/) in Kendal has a cinema, music and comedy. Our [things to do](/things-to-do/) page has the full list.

          ## Eating In and Eating Out

          Both six-berth holiday cottages have well-equipped kitchens, though one guest found the kitchen at Farrier's Cottage on the small side, and one Roger Pot guest called it the best equipped house they had ever stayed in. [The Meat Hook](/places/the-meat-hook-sedbergh/) in Sedbergh is a family-run butcher, [Powells](/places/powells-sedbergh/) sells fruit and vegetables, and Tesco, Morrisons, Asda and Sainsbury's all deliver to the cottages, which saves a big shop on the way up. See our [home deliveries](/places/home-deliveries/) page.

          The pubs are a drive away, in Sedbergh, Hawes, Hardraw and Dentdale, and our [eating and drinking](/eating-and-drinking-in-garsdale/) page lists them. There are normally no taxis in Garsdale, and [On Time Taxis](/places/on-time-taxis-sedbergh/) in Sedbergh works daytimes with a single four-seater car, so for a night out somebody will need to drive.

          ## Good to Know Before You Book

          Roger Pot is reached by a 400 metre farm track with two or three gates to open for the owner's horses. It is single width, steep and uneven in places, and the owners run a simple texting system so that nobody meets halfway. A normal car manages it, but very low profile cars are better left on the road. If your group is coming in three cars, ask Paul about parking when you book.

          The water at Roger Pot comes from a well that is tested and treated every year, and bottled water is supplied for drinking. The garden at Farrier's Cottage is not enclosed and a public right of way crosses the front. The nearest shops are in Sedbergh, six to seven miles away.
