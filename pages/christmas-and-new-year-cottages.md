          ## Christmas Holiday Cottages in Garsdale

          Our three self-catering holiday cottages in Garsdale, in the Yorkshire Dales National Park near Sedbergh, are let year round, including Christmas and New Year, subject to each owner's calendar. [Roger Pot](/properties/roger-pot/) sleeps six, has a woodburning stove and takes up to two dogs with no charge for them. [Farrier's Cottage](/properties/farriers-cottage/) sleeps six in a stone cottage that dates back to 1675, with a stove in a big exposed-stone fireplace. [The Old Cart House](/properties/the-old-cart-house/) sleeps two, for a couple who want a quiet Christmas away.

          Garsdale is a valley of farms and stone cottages strung along the A684 rather than a village with a high street, so there are no Christmas lights and nothing much open late. The cottages are warm, the stoves get lit, there are sheep in the fields along the River Clough and, some years, snow on Baugh Fell. It gets dark at around four in the afternoon in late December, so the evenings are long, and Roger Pot has plenty of board games, which one New Year group said kept them entertained when the weather kept them in. Guests who have stayed at Roger Pot over Christmas and New Year have written about the house being decorated, the welcome hamper and, in one case, arriving to find the woodburning stove already lit.

          ## Christmas and New Year at Roger Pot

          [Roger Pot](/properties/roger-pot/) is a detached stone holiday cottage a quarter of a mile up a farm track, with three bedrooms, two bathrooms and a sitting room with exposed stone walls, roof beams and a woodburning stove. Guests who have stayed over Christmas and New Year have found the house decorated, and there is a basket of logs for the woodburning stove when you arrive, with Paul next door able to let you have more, at cost, if you get through them. The downstairs has underfloor heating, which one January guest called "a real surprise" on a cold, windy weekend.

          Paul and Karen live in the barn next door, and Paul bakes for every guest, so the welcome hamper in December is the same homemade bread and cake that people write about the rest of the year, with milk, eggs and the basics in the fridge. The dining room is separate from the kitchen and the table extends, which is handy at Christmas if family staying elsewhere in the dale come over for dinner, though one New Year group found their visitors had to bring their own chairs.

          The house sleeps six, and the twin room can be made up as a super-king if you ask Paul in advance, so it works for three couples as well as for a family. A highchair is available, and the utility room has a hard floor that suits wet dogs.

          ## A Christmas for Two at The Old Cart House

          [The Old Cart House](/properties/the-old-cart-house/) is a one-bedroom stone cottage that sleeps two, converted from an old cart house and stable by Jack and Loraine, who renovated Rose Cottage next door before starting on it. It has a king-size bed, an en-suite shower room, exposed beams under a vaulted ceiling and an electric fire downstairs, and the electric central heating keeps it warm when the weather turns. It sits on the A684 with parking at the roadside, so there is no farm track to worry about if it snows, and the river runs just over the road.

          There is a basket of local treats waiting for every guest when you arrive, and Jack and Loraine take one-night bookings as well as longer ones, so a night or two away before Christmas is easy to arrange online. One pet is welcome.

          ## Farrier's Cottage at Christmas

          [Farrier's Cottage](/properties/farriers-cottage/) began life in 1675 as the blacksmith's cottage and workshop, and it is furnished with antiques, including a grandfather clock in the dining room and a table that seats six. The sitting room has a woodburning stove set in a big exposed-stone fireplace, fuel for the stove is included, and there is gas central heating as well. It sleeps six across a king-size double, a second double and a twin.

          Phil, who owns it, takes enquiries through the form on the cottage page and aims to reply within 72 hours, so for Christmas or New Year it is worth asking well ahead rather than leaving it to the last week.

          ## Christmas Food and Shopping

          The nearest shops are in [Sedbergh](/locations/sedbergh/), six to seven miles down the dale, where there is a Spar, [Powells](/places/powells-sedbergh/) for fruit and vegetables and [The Meat Hook](/places/the-meat-hook-sedbergh/), a family-run butcher where it makes sense to order your Christmas meat ahead. Hawes is nine miles the other way, and [Kendal](/locations/kendal/), about twenty-five minutes away, has the big supermarkets. Tesco, Morrisons, Asda and Sainsbury's all deliver to the cottages, though Christmas slots go early, and at Roger Pot the vans are met at the gate because they are too big for the track. Our [home deliveries](/places/home-deliveries/) page has the details.

          Expect nothing in the dale to be open on Christmas Day, and if you want Christmas dinner at a pub in Sedbergh or Hawes it is worth booking well ahead. The kitchens at all three cottages are well equipped, and Roger Pot and Farrier's Cottage, the two that sleep six, both have a dishwasher.

          ## Things to Do Over Christmas and New Year

          A Boxing Day walk can start at the door. The bridleway beside Dandra Garth goes up through the woods towards Dent and the River Clough has paths along it, and the [Sedgwick Geological Trail](/the-sedgwick-geological-trail/) at Danny Bridge, a few miles down the A684 towards Sedbergh, is a riverside walk of about 600 metres that suits a short winter day. Our [walks page](/walks/) has more, and our [winter cottage breaks](/winter-cottage-breaks/) page covers walking in snow and short daylight.

          Sedbergh holds a Christmas late-night shopping evening each December, and the Dentdale Choir sings carol concerts in Dent and Cowgill in the run-up to Christmas. Our [local events](/events/local-events/) page has more, with links to this year's Sedbergh and Dentdale Choir listings. [Hardraw Force](/places/hardraw-force/) is worth seeing after heavy rain, the [Wensleydale Creamery](/places/wensleydale-creamery-hawes/) in Hawes sells cheese for the Christmas board, and the [Settle-Carlisle railway](/places/garsdale-station/) runs from Garsdale Station over the holidays, though there are no trains on Christmas Day, usually none on Boxing Day, and engineering work sometimes puts buses on in place of trains between Christmas and New Year, so check the timetable.

          New Year in Garsdale is quiet, and on a clear night the sky over the dale is dark enough to see the Milky Way from the cottage garden.
