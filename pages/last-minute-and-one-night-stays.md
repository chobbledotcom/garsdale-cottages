          ## Last Minute Holiday Cottages in Garsdale

          A lot of holiday cottages in the Yorkshire Dales and Cumbria ask for two or three nights at least, which is no use if you are breaking a drive up the M6, walking a long-distance path, or already in the Dales and need a bed for tonight. [The Old Cart House](/properties/the-old-cart-house/), our one-bedroom stone cottage for two in Garsdale near Sedbergh, takes one-night stays, and you can book it online. If the availability calendar on the cottage page lets you book for tonight, you may be able to get a same-day booking.

          [Roger Pot](/properties/roger-pot/), which sleeps six, also books online, so it is worth checking its calendar, but short-notice and one-night stays there are not guaranteed and minimum stays may apply, so ring Paul first. [Farrier's Cottage](/properties/farriers-cottage/) takes enquiries rather than online bookings, so it is better for stays planned a little ahead.

          Go to [The Old Cart House](/properties/the-old-cart-house/) page and open the availability calendar. If you are going to [Roger Pot](/properties/roger-pot/), where Paul and Karen live in the barn next door, let Paul know when you expect to arrive, since the 400 metre farm track is single width with gates and he runs a texting system so cars do not meet on it.

          The postcode is shared with other houses in Garsdale and will not take you to the door, so use the directions the owners send you. Mobile signal in Garsdale is patchy, so save the directions to your phone before you lose coverage.

          ## For Walkers on a Long-Distance Path

          The cottages are not on the big long-distance paths themselves, but the Pennine Way, the Dales Way and the Coast to Coast all pass within a short drive. The Pennine Way goes through [Hawes](/locations/hawes/), nine miles east, the Dales Way runs through Dent and past [Sedbergh](/locations/sedbergh/), and Wainwright's Coast to Coast passes through [Kirkby Stephen](/locations/kirkby-stephen/), about twenty minutes north. The Pennine Bridleway crosses the hillside above Garsdale Head, and you can pick it up from the path behind the Moorcock Inn. After a day on the trail, The Old Cart House has a wide walk-in shower, a king-size bed and a washing machine for the kit.

          Getting to and from the trail takes some planning. There are normally no taxis in Garsdale. [On Time Taxis](/places/on-time-taxis-sedbergh/) in Sedbergh runs one four-seater car in the daytime, and the Little White Bus runs from [Garsdale Station](/places/garsdale-station/) to Hawes, but there is no public transport from the station into Garsdale itself. If you are coming by train on the Settle-Carlisle line, check with Jack and Loraine how you will get from the station to the cottage before you book.

          ## For Road Trips and Long Drives

          If you are driving between the south and Scotland, Garsdale is around twenty minutes off the M6 at junction 37, along the A684 through Sedbergh, and our [20 Minutes from the M6](/20-minutes-from-the-m6/) page has the route. From the A1, the A684 comes over from Leyburn and Hawes, and our [getting here](/getting-here/) page covers both. The Old Cart House has electric vehicle charging if you need it, though Jack and Loraine ask you to talk to them about it on arrival, as there may be a small charge.

          For a touring holiday, one night here can be the start of a longer loop, with the Buttertubs Pass into Swaledale, Wensleydale, the Howgill Fells and the [Lake District](/places/lake-district/) all within a day's drive and back.

          ## Arriving at Short Notice

          The nearest shops are in Sedbergh, about 6.4 miles away, and nothing in the dale is open late, so if you are arriving in the evening, pick up something for tea and breakfast on the way. Jack and Loraine leave a basket of local treats with tea and coffee for every guest, but it is mostly treats, so bring milk and something for tea and breakfast, especially on a same-day booking. The nearest pubs are a short drive away in Sedbergh and Hawes, and our [eating and drinking](/eating-and-drinking-in-garsdale/) page lists them.

          The Old Cart House sleeps two, or a couple with a baby, and takes one pet. The bedroom and shower room are upstairs, and there is no bathroom on the ground floor.
