# Garsdale & Sedbergh: Tourist Information from the Parish Council Archives

Compiled from archived pages of the Garsdale Parish Council website (garsdaleparishcouncil.com), sourced via the Wayback Machine.

---

## Wildlife and Nature

- **Otters on the River Clough** -- Signs of otters (spraints and footprints) have been confirmed along the banks of the River Clough in Garsdale. The Environment Agency's 2010 survey of the Lune Catchment found positive otter signs at 70 of 100 sites, including the Clough -- a dramatic increase from just 37 sites in 2007. The otters have naturally re-colonised the area (they were not re-introduced), which is considered a good indicator of how much habitats and water quality have improved.

- **Red Squirrels** -- Red squirrels are present in the Garsdale valley. The Northern Red Squirrels Group has been active in the area, and warning signs for drivers have been put up along the valley road, as road kill is the main threat to the local population. Feeders and nest boxes have been discussed to help squirrels survive harsh winters.

- **Migratory Fish and Eels** -- A large culvert was installed at Winterscales Farm on the Clough (replacing an easily blocked pipe bridge), funded by the Lune Rivers Trust, to allow migratory fish and eels to pass upstream -- rejuvenating river ecology.

- **Invasive Plants** -- Japanese Knotweed and Himalayan Balsam have been spreading along the banks of the Clough River. Himalayan Balsam is described as "an attractive plant" despite being invasive -- walkers along the riverbanks may notice it.

- **Squirrel Fest** -- An annual red squirrel awareness event held at the Lake District National Park Visitor Centre, Brockhole, Windermere. Described as "a fun day out in a beautiful place" with family activities and red squirrel information.

## Landscape and Scenic Views

- **Baugh Fell** -- A prominent fell visible from much of Garsdale, featured repeatedly in the parish website's photo gallery with images including "A December morning across Baugh Fell", "Sunset on Baugh Fell", and "Stars Above Baugh Fell" (the latter by Rachel Kosciuszko, suggesting excellent dark skies for stargazing).

- **Garsdale and Grisedale** -- Described on the parish website as being "set in one of the most beautiful parts of Cumbria." Grisedale is a remote neighbouring valley.

- **Named Landscape Features** -- The A684 runs through the dale past named locations including Black Horse Hill, Dandra Garth, Badger Dub, Longstone Fell, Aye Gill, and Roger Pot -- useful landmarks for walkers and visitors orientating themselves.

- **Yorkshire Dales National Park** -- All of Garsdale falls within the Yorkshire Dales National Park, confirming its status as a protected landscape of outstanding quality.

## The Railway

- **Garsdale Station (Garsdale Head)** -- A stop on the famous Settle-Carlisle railway line, located at Garsdale Head. The station is served by Network Rail and is within the Yorkshire Dales National Park.

- **Dandrymire / Moorcock Viaduct** -- A notable railway viaduct on the Settle-Carlisle line near Garsdale Head. A landmark engineering feature visible from the dale.

- **"A Pennine Journey -- from Settle to Hadrian's Wall"** -- Repeatedly referenced on the parish council homepage as a featured link, indicating the Settle-Carlisle corridor and this long-distance route are prominent local attractions.

## Pubs and Local Venues

- **The Moorcock Inn** -- A pub at Garsdale Head that regularly hosted pub quiz nights on Tuesday evenings, starting at 8.30pm (entry £2 per person, with 10% of proceeds donated to the air ambulance). The quizmaster was Neville Woodfine. The BBC also filmed at the Moorcock for the Sunday Politics Show. A social hub for the dale.

- **The George and Dragon** -- A local pub mentioned as a filming location for a BBC Sunday Politics Show piece in 2012.

- **Stoneclose Tea Room, Dent** -- Referenced as a business in nearby Dentdale, with a link to dentdale.com.

## Garsdale Village Hall

- Described as "the only social meeting place left in Garsdale." Available for community use and hire. Located next to Kirk Bridge. Annual running costs approximately £2,000. Used for bonfire night refreshments, jubilee celebrations, annual sports teas, and other community events. Has an outdoor field used for larger events.

## Local Events and Traditions

- **Garsdale Bonfire Night** -- An annual community bonfire, usually held on a Friday near 5 November (sometimes moved to Saturday to avoid clashing with Sedbergh's bonfire). Starts at 7.00pm. Entry: £2.50 for adults, children free. Hot soup and hot dogs served in the Village Hall afterwards. A well-established community tradition.

- **Annual Garsdale Sports** -- A traditional children's sports day held on the Village Hall field, typically starting at 1.00pm, followed by tea in the Village Hall. Has been running for many years.

- **Queen's Diamond Jubilee Celebrations (2012)** -- A full day of events: children's sports from 1pm, tea in the Village Hall, then an evening marquee on the Village Hall field with hog roast, homemade desserts, disco with bar, grand raffle, and optional fancy dress (with prize for best outfit). Tickets were £10 adults, £5 primary-age children.

- **Sedbergh Spring Show** -- An annual agricultural and community show held at the Peoples Hall, Sedbergh (e.g. Saturday 23 April 2016). Entry forms available free from shops in Sedbergh.

- **Pub Quizzes at the Moorcock** -- Regular monthly quiz nights, typically on Tuesday evenings. A fun community social event open to all.

## Local History

- **"Bell Pratt of Grisdale"** -- A local history book about a man born in 1910 in Grisdale, covering farming life in the dale, army service, Methodist lay preaching, life in Sedbergh, Leeds and Kendal, and the farming year. Described as "a delightful book" giving "a real feel for those times" -- "a book you can dip in and out of and learn a lot about the past and the history of this area."

- **"Aygill to Foldgill"** -- The parish website's History section featured content by Kevin Lancaster covering local history and place names across the dale, from Aygill to Foldgill.

- **Kirk Bridge** -- A listed bridge adjacent to the Village Hall, subject to conservation concern when repairs used stone of the wrong type and colour. Its listed status confirms its heritage significance.

- **BBC Sunday Politics Show (2012)** -- Garsdale was featured on BBC1's Sunday Politics Show, with filming at Garsdale Design and the George and Dragon pub, giving the area a national profile.

## Historic and Heritage Structures

- **Kirk Bridge** -- A listed bridge near the Village Hall, of heritage interest.
- **Banks Farm, Garsdale** -- A listed farm building (subject to listed building consent for conversion), representative of traditional Dales farm architecture.
- **East Scale, Grisdale** -- A historic former dwelling in Grisdale, subject to reinstatement planning and listed building consent.

## Nearby Businesses

- **Garsdale Design Ltd** -- An RIBA-chartered architecture, heritage and urban design practice based at High Branthwaites, Garsdale Road, Frostrow, Sedbergh LA10 5JR (tel: 015396 20875). Featured on BBC television. An example of professional creative businesses operating in this rural community.
