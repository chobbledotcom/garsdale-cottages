# Garsdale Red Squirrels - Tourist Information

Source: archived pages from garsdaleredsquirrels.org.uk (circa 2014-2015)

## Red Squirrels in Garsdale

Garsdale is home to a thriving population of red squirrels -- one of the last groups of reds left in the Yorkshire Dales. The Garsdale Red Squirrel Group was formed in 2014 to help protect them. By early 2015 the group numbered 38 local members.

The red squirrels are under threat from habitat loss due to forestry clear felling and from the invasive grey squirrel, which carries the deadly Squirrel Pox Virus. The coniferous forest areas are the prime red squirrel habitat, as grey squirrels tend to prosper in broadleaf/deciduous woodland along the river corridor.

## About the Dale

Garsdale is a quiet valley in the north-west corner of the Yorkshire Dales National Park. It runs from Sedbergh to Garsdale Head, with the A684 road and the River Clough following the dale floor through pasture and meadow land.

Surrounding valleys and landmarks:
- Grisedale runs north-east towards the Rawthey valley
- Mallerstang lies north from Garsdale Head
- Dentdale is to the south
- Baugh Fell rises to the north
- Rise Hill lies to the south
- Steep hillsides climb to high peat moorland

## Seeing Red Squirrels

Visitors to Garsdale may be lucky enough to spot red squirrels in the woodlands along the valley. The nearby Snaizeholme Red Squirrel Trail (run by the Yorkshire Dales Biodiversity Forum) offers a dedicated red squirrel viewing experience in Wensleydale.
