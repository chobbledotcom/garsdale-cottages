      ## Sedbergh and Local Events


      Sedbergh has a small weekly market on Wednesday mornings and during summer
      there is an artisan market once a month. Sedbergh has a regular events
      calendar throughout the year and some of this year's events are the Spring
      Show on 21st March, the Young Farmers tractor pull up Winder Hill on 13
      June and [Sedbergh Music Festival](https://sedberghmusicfestival.com/)
      from 16-26 June. There is also the Christmas late night shopping event in
      December. In addition there are regular exhibitions and events at Farfield
      Mill. Local events can be found in the Sedbergh and district Lookaround
      monthly magazine, usually available in the Spar shop and other shops
      locally or online at
      [sedberghlookaround.org.uk](https://sedberghlookaround.org.uk/). For more
      Sedbergh events see
      [sedbergh.org.uk](https://www.sedbergh.org.uk/events/).

      ## Hawes and Wensleydale


      Hawes has a small weekly market on Tuesday mornings along with regular
      craft fairs and events in the market hall. Hawes also has a regular
      cattle, farming and livestock auction most weeks — see the
      [Hawes Mart sales diary](https://www.hawesmart.co.uk/sales-diary) for
      dates.
  - type: markdown
    content: >-
      ## Cumbria and the Lake District


      Cumbria has many annual events in the nearby towns of Kendal, Ambleside,
      Bowness and Windermere, for example the Westmorland County Show, Barbon
      Sheep Show and music festivals such as Kendal Calling, and slightly
      further away such as [Potfest in the Park](https://potfest.co.uk/visit/potfest-in-the-park/)
      24-26 July at Hutton in the Forest and Keswick Film Festival. For more
      see [What's on in Cumbria](https://www.visitcumbria.com/whats-on/).
  - type: markdown
    content: >-
      ## North Yorkshire


      We are on the border of North Yorkshire so we have the benefit of events
      such as Medieval Music in the Dales at Bolton Castle Sept 11-13 and the
      Wensleydale Show on 29 August 2026. There are regular country fairs
      throughout summer in many of the towns and villages, for example: Muker
      Show, Barbon Sheep Show, Moorcock Show, Wensleydale Show, Potfest,
      Westmorland Show and many more. See [country shows and festivals](https://www.lakedistrict.gov.uk/visiting/things-to-do/beyond-the-trails/country-shows-and-festivals)
      for listings, and [Visit North Yorkshire](https://visitnorthyorkshire.com/)
      for more events across the region.
