---
title: Local Events
subtitle: What's on in Sedbergh, Cumbria and surrounding areas.
---
Sedbergh has a regular events calender throughout the year and some of this years events are the Spring Show on 21st March,the Young farmers tractor pull up Winder Hill on 13 June and Sedbergh Music festival from 16-26 June. In addition there are regular exhibitions and events at Farfield Mill and throughout summer there is an artisan market once a month.
Sedbergh events ([sedbergh.org.uk](https://www.sedbergh.org.uk/events/))
Sedbergh Music Festival ([sedberghmusicfestival.com](https://sedberghmusicfestival.com/))

Cumbria has many annual events in the nearby towns of Kendal, Ambleside , Bowness and Windermere for example the Westmorland County Show, Barbon Sheep Show and music festivals such as Kendal Calling, and slightly further away such as Potfest in the Park 24-26 July at Hutton in the Forest and Keswick film festival.
Potfest in the Park ([potfest.co.uk](https://potfest.co.uk/visit/potfest-in-the-park/))
What's on in Cumbria ([visitcumbria.com](https://www.visitcumbria.com/whats-on/))

We are on the border of North Yorkshire so we have the benefit of events such as Medieval Music in the Dales at Bolton Castle Sept 11-13 and the Wensleydale Show on 29 August 2026. Hawes also has a weekly market and often craft fairs in the market hall.
Visit North Yorkshire ([visitnorthyorkshire.com](https://visitnorthyorkshire.com/))
