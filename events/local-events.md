  content: '## Sedbergh Artisan Markets


    Sedbergh holds a small weekly produce market every Wednesday morning on Joss Lane.
    Through the summer months this is joined by a monthly artisan market on the last
    Saturday of the month, with craft stalls, street food and live music along Main

    with a morning''s walk on Winder or a look around Sedbergh''s independent
    bookshops.

    On Saturday 24 April 2027 the Dentdale Choir gives its spring concert at St Andrew''s

- type: split-image
  content: '## Getting Around Without a Car


    The DalesBus 832 Sunday service between Sedbergh and Kirkby Lonsdale was pulled
    off the road part way through 2026 by the Garsdale road closures, and the DalesBus
    team hopes to reinstate it for 2027 subject to funding and an operator - check
    [dalesbus.org](https://www.dalesbus.org/) before travelling. Western Dales Bus
    stepped in with its S7 service for the rest of that season, so a Sunday run over
    to Kirkby Lonsdale for lunch is often still possible. The Little White Bus also
    links Garsdale station with Hawes, timed to meet certain Settle-Carlisle trains -
    see [littlewhitebus.co.uk](https://www.littlewhitebus.co.uk/) for the current
    timetable.
