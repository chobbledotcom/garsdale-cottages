    *2.5 miles/4km | 1.5 hours | easy*

    Dentdale is a U-shaped glacial valley running east from Dent village, and it contains some of Britain's surviving upland hay meadows - traditionally farmed to allow wildlife to thrive, and blanketed in wildflowers in spring and summer. This short circular walk starts from the narrow, cobbled streets of Dent village, climbs to Gawthorp with views across the dale, then returns along the banks of the River Dee, following a section of the Dales Way through riverside pastures and hay meadows.

    Dent itself is a small village with cobbled streets and stone houses, about 25 minutes' drive from Garsdale. The [Dent Village Heritage Centre and Museum](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/) is worth a look, and there is the Dent Brewery, whose beers you will find in the pubs around the Dales. The walk is easy enough for families, and the hay meadows are at their best in June and July.
