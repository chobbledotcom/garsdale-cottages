    SPAR Hawes is a small supermarket on Market Place in the centre of Hawes, handy for picking up groceries and basic supplies if you are spending the day in Wensleydale. It sells the usual convenience-store range - bread, milk, eggs, basic groceries, newspapers - and it has a cash machine, which is useful in a town where banks have mostly gone. The shop is right on the market square, alongside [The Chippie](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/the-chippie/) and [Caffe Curva](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/caff-curva/), so it is easy to pair with a lunch or a cup of tea.

    ## Details

    - **Address:** Market Place, Hawes, North Yorkshire, DL8 3QX
