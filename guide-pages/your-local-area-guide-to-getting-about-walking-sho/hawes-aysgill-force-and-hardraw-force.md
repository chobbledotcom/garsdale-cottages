    *7 miles/11.3km | 5 hours | moderate*

    This walk from Hawes visits three of the Yorkshire Dales' waterfalls - Hardraw Force, Cotter Force and Aysgill Force - following the River Ure and the Pennine Way through Wensleydale. Hardraw Force is England's highest single-drop waterfall, a reputed 100-foot drop that you reach through the grounds of the [Green Dragon Inn](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/the-green-dragon-inn/) at Hardraw. There is a small admission fee to the waterfall. Aysgill Force is a narrower, quieter fall further up the valley, reached via a path from Gayle.

    The route uses the Pennine Way and other public footpaths, starting and ending in Hawes, which has the [White Hart Inn](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/the-white-hart-inn-hawes/), [The Chippie](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/the-chippie/) and [Caffe Curva](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/caff-curva/) for food and drink before or after. The [Dales Countryside Museum](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/dales-countryside-museum/) and the [Wensleydale Creamery](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/wensleydale-creamery-visitor-centre/) are both in Hawes if you want to make a full day of it. Hawes is about 30 minutes' drive from Garsdale.

    ## Walk details

    - **Start point:** Hawes (Market Place, DL8 3QX)
    - **Distance:** 7 miles/11.3km
    - **Parking:** Market Place or [SPAR Hawes](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/spar-hawes/) car park
