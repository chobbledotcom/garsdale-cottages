    The NatWest branch in Kirkby Lonsdale closed in 2025, part of the wider pattern of bank branch closures across rural Cumbria and the Dales. NatWest published a branch closure document for Kirkby Lonsdale as part of its 2025 closure programme, so there is no longer a NatWest counter or ATM here.

    For cash in Kirkby Lonsdale, the [Market Square](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/market-square-kirkby-lonsdale/) has free-to-use cash machines, and several shops offer cashback. For NatWest counter services, the nearest open branch is in Kendal. The [Post Office](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/sedbergh-post-office/) at SPAR Sedbergh also offers basic banking services for several banks including NatWest.
