    Jhoots Pharmacy was a pharmacy on Main Street in Sedbergh, at number 48-50. It closed without warning on 21 August 2025, citing a lack of a pharmacist. The BBC reported that the closure left people in Sedbergh facing a ten-mile journey to the nearest pharmacy.

    While Jhoots is shut, the nearest pharmacies are [Central Dales Pharmacy in Hawes](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/central-dales-pharmacy-hawes/) (about twenty minutes' drive east) and pharmacies in Kendal. For minor ailments and over-the-counter medication, [SPAR Sedbergh](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/spar-sedbergh/) on Station Road sells a basic range. For prescriptions and urgent medicine, [Westmorland General Hospital](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/westmorland-general-hospital-kendal/) in Kendal has an Urgent Treatment Centre.
