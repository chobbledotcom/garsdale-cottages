    *6.8 miles/11km | 4 hours | moderate*

    Muker is a small village in Upper Swaledale, and its landscape is largely unchanged from the late 18th and early 19th century - the field barns and drystone walls radiate out from the village in the pattern that has been there for two hundred years. Muker is also famous for its species-rich hay meadows, which burst into colour in late spring and early summer. This circular route starts at Muker and follows the River Swale east to the village of Gunnerside, returning across the fells.

    Swaledale is a lush green valley with a patchwork of tiny fields and barns, and it has never been one of the more popular tourist dales, which is part of its appeal. The walk takes in the area at its best and most dramatic. Muker is about 45 minutes' drive from Garsdale, over the tops into Swaledale. The [Tan Hill Inn](/guide/your-local-area-guide-to-getting-about-walking-sho/keld-to-tan-hill-inn/) - Britain's highest pub - is reachable on a longer walk from Keld, further up the dale.
