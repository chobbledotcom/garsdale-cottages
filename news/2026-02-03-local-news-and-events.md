Sedbergh has a small weekly market on Wednesday mornings and during summer there is an artisan market once a month.
Sedbergh has a Spring Show and also Sedbergh Music Festival along with the Christmas late night shopping event in December.
Local events can be found in the Sedbergh and district Lookaround monthly magazine, usually available in the Spar shop and other shops locally or online.
https://sedberghlookaround.org.uk/
https://www.sedbergh.org.uk/events/
Hawes has a small weekly market on Tuesday mornings along with regular craft fairs and events in the market hall.
Hawes has a regular cattle, farming  and livestock auction most weeks.
https://www.hawesmart.co.uk/sales-diary
There are regular country fairs throughout summer in many of the towns and villages, for example: Muker show, Barbon sheep show, Moorcock show, Wensleydale show,Potfest, Westmorland Show and many more.
https://www.lakedistrict.gov.uk/visiting/things-to-do/beyond-the-trails/country-shows-and-festivals
